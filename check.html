Can an AI assistant sell your product?

Ten yes-or-no questions for anyone who sells online. You end with a short list of fixes, the most important one first.

About 15 minutes with your site open in another tab Nothing to sign up for. Your answers stay in this browser Every check is free and needs no developer

Shoppers are starting to ask an assistant instead of searching. Google now runs one cart across Search and Gemini. Walmart lets approved outside agents buy for its customers. Meta, Anthropic and others have released assistants that can check out with the shopper's OK.

None of them can look at your product the way a person does. They read what is written down. When the price, stock or name is missing, or differs from one place to the next, they skip you or get you wrong. Most of the fixes are small, and they help ordinary search and real customers too.

    What this is and isn't

    A self-check, not a measurement. It tells you whether the basics an assistant relies on are in place. It can't tell you how often an assistant recommends you, and it doesn't predict sales. Very few purchases happen through agents today. The reason to fix these now is that they're cheap, they help today's search and customers, and the businesses that are easy to describe accurately are the ones assistants will pick as this grows.
